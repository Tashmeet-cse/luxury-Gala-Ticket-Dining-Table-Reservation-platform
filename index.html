Frequently Asked Questions

What is the official Gala dress code?
The dress code is strictly Black Tie and Floor-Length Evening Gown. Tuxedos or formal dinner jackets with bow ties are required for gentlemen. Athletic wear, casual suits, or sneakers are not permitted.
Can our party select dietary restrictions or allergies?
Yes. During checkout, you can specify individual dietary requirements, kosher/halal preferences, and allergies for every guest at your table.
What is the cancellation and refund policy?
Reservations are fully refundable up to 14 days prior to December 31, 2026. Within 14 days, reservations can be transferred to another name via our concierge team.
How does the table hold and reservation work?
When you select a table on the floor plan and initiate booking, that table is temporarily locked for 10 minutes to prevent double-booking while you complete your reservation details.
